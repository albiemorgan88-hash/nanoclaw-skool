## Verification caveat

Direct page fetches (WebFetch) are egress-blocked for essentially every job board in this environment. Every detail below — salary, remote status, posted date, eligibility — is reconstructed from **search-result snippets**, not from reading the listing page. Confidence ratings reflect exactly that: **High** means pay and status were corroborated across sources, **Medium** means the key facts appeared in a snippet but were not cross-checked, **Low** means treat every detail as unverified until the link is opened. Open the link before acting on any number.

**Posting dates are the weakest field in this run.** Snippets almost never carried one; `postedDate` is null wherever it was not stated rather than guessed. Several listings may already be closed.

**Zero Gmail drafts were created this run.** Not one of the 115 unique listings published a contact email address — every single one routes through an application form or a recruiter portal. That is the normal outcome for board-sourced roles, and it means there is nothing sitting in the mailbox awaiting a send.
