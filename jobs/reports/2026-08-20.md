## Verification caveat

Direct page fetches (WebFetch) are egress-blocked for essentially every job board in this environment. Every detail below — salary, remote status, posted date, eligibility — is reconstructed from **search-result snippets**, not from reading the listing page. Confidence ratings reflect that: **High** means the pay and status were corroborated across sources, **Low** means treat the details as unverified until the link is opened. Open the link before acting on any number.

**No listing in this sweep published a contact email address**, so zero Gmail drafts were created this run. That is a normal outcome for board-sourced roles — every one routes through an application form.
